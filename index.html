Who This Is For

Nobody should have to choose between getting to work and putting food on the table. Here is who this initiative serves.

For Organizations & MDAs

Public and private sector bodies seeking verified ride support for staff.

For Individuals & Homes

Families and individuals navigating higher transport costs daily.

For Schools

Students and staff who need safe, affordable transport to and from school.

For NGOs

Non-profits reaching communities with practical, sustainable transport relief.

Why We Are Collecting Data

We are asking for data for the purpose of researching the condition and effect on Nigerian organizations and homes after fuel subsidy removal.

  • Your responses are anonymous — we cannot identify you.

  • Data is used for research only. We do not sell or share it.

  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing us at olandasolutions@gmail.com.

How It Works

Three simple steps from response to real-world support.

1

Take the Survey

3-minute bus stop intercept or 8-minute MDA staff survey. Completely anonymous.

2

We Analyze

We analyze responses anonymously, stratified by corridor and employment type.

3

We Share Findings + Support

We publish findings and pilot practical transport support (e.g., office-level wallets).
