Our Story

Stories from the road, from the ground, from Nigeria.

In May 2023, Nigeria removed its petrol subsidy. What followed was not just a change in policy — it was a quiet crisis unfolding in homes, offices, schools, and communities across the country.

Fuel prices jumped from N195 to over N900 per litre. Transport costs surged. For millions of Nigerians, getting to work, school, or the market became a daily negotiation between survival and dignity.

We started this initiative to listen. To document. To understand how transport — the invisible thread that connects Nigerian life — was being stretched to breaking point.

Our Mission

Turn research into practical, dignified transport support.

We believe that better transport is not a luxury — it is a foundation. Our mission is to:

  • Listen to Nigerians about their transport reality.
  • Research how subsidy removal reshaped daily life.
  • Pilot practical solutions — like verified ride-share and office transport wallets.
  • Share findings openly so policy and practice can improve.
