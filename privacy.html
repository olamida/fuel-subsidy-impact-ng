Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At the Transport Support Initiative, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. What Data We Collect

Our survey collects the following non-identifying data:

  • Corridor of primary transport use
  • Employment type
  • Approximate monthly transport cost (optional)
  • Transport cost changes (e.g., increase in %)
  • Willingness to pay more for verified ride-share
  • Trust-building preferences
  • Employer transport support willingness
  • Optional comments

We do not collect: names, phone numbers, email addresses, or any other information that could identify you.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Analyze transport cost trends after fuel subsidy removal
  • Understand coping strategies of organizations, homes, schools, and NGOs
  • Pilot practical transport support programs (e.g., verified ride-share, office transport wallets)
  • Share anonymized findings with policymakers and partners

4. Data Security

Survey responses are stored in encrypted Google Forms linked to a password-protected Google Sheet. Only authorized team members have access. Data is aggregated and anonymized before any public reporting.

5. Your Rights

  • Withdraw consent: Email olandasolutions@gmail.com and we will delete your data.
  • Access: Contact us for a copy of your anonymized responses.
  • Opt-out: You can stop participating at any time.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, write to us at olandasolutions@gmail.com. We respond within 2 business days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page.
